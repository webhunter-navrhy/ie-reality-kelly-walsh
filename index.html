Set within the prestigious Woodlands Grove development, this penthouse stands out as one of Blackrock’s most impressive new homes. This 3-bedroom penthouse is extending to approx. 178 sq.m / 1,916 sq.ft.

Fully designed and fitted out by renowned interior designer Helen Turkington, commanding a rare sense of privacy, luxury and refinement, with every detail crafted to the highest specification. The property is offered fully furnished with all contents included, presenting a turn key opportunity for the purchaser.

The interior design is elegant and refined. The penthouse features a spacious open-plan living, dining and kitchen area with bespoke contemporary cabinetry, Quartz Calacatta Gold worktops, integrated Miele appliances and a wine fridge.

Floor to ceiling glazing and a dual aspect flood the interior with natural light, while two generous private terraces, one opening from the dining area and the other from the master bedroom, provide exceptional outdoor space. Finished with porcelain decking and feature lighting, both terraces offer ideal retreats overlooking the landscaped grounds.

The bedrooms are luxuriously finished, with the master suite featuring a large en-suite and stunning walk in wardrobe complete with high quality fitted storage. Bedroom two also benefits from a generously sized walk in wardrobe, adding to the home’s exceptional level of design. Bathrooms and en-suites offer sleek tiling, rain-head showers, heated towel rails and premium sanitaryware throughout.

With an A2 energy rating, air-source heat pump, zoned heating and superior glazing, the property delivers excellent energy efficiency. Residents enjoy secure parking, lift access, CCTV, video intercom, and number-plate recognition entry, all set within beautifully landscaped communal gardens. The penthouse blends exceptional comfort with cutting-edge modern living.

Ideally located between Blackrock Village and the N11, Woodlands Grove offers exceptional transport links by road, bus and DART, as well as immediate access to the area’s renowned shopping, dining and coastal amenities.
